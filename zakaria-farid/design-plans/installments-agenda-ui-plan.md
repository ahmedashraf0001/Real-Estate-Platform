# أجندة ومواعيد الأقساط وسندات القبض — UI and implementation handoff

**Status:** Proposal for review; no product code or accounting behavior changed. **Audience:** FIN-OS collections operator / cashier / finance manager. **Mode:** Operate, not a dashboard or marketing page. **Visual authority:** Preserve the current docked FIN-OS shell, Arabic RTL, white neutral surfaces, live accent palette, canonical metric cards, tables, and modal shells. Do not implement either discarded ledger mockup.

## 1. Business model and source of truth

- A contract (`ERPContract`) owns a payment schedule (`ERPInstallmentSchedule`). `ContractsEngine.generateSchedule` produces a down payment at tranche 0 and later installments at 1..N; the final tranche absorbs rounding to preserve the exact contract amount (`src/lib/erp/contracts.ts:15-129`). Display tranche 0 as **دفعة الحجز والمقدم**, not as an ordinary later installment.
- Schedule fields: `contract_id`, `schedule_id`, `tranche_number`, `nominal_value`, `due_date`, `amount_paid`, `paid_date`, `status` (`Pending`, `Paid`, `Partially Paid`, `Defaulted`, `SUPERSEDED`, `Void`) (`src/lib/erp/types.ts:115-135`). Do not show superseded/void entries as actionable dues.
- A PDC / instrument (`ERPPDCRecord`) may link to a schedule through `schedule_id` and has its own due date and lifecycle `In Safe`, `Deposited`, `Cleared`, `Bounced`, `Void` (`types.ts:137-151`). **Deposited is not collected**; only confirmed clearance/payment belongs in collected amounts. A contract schedule can exist without a PDC: `HandCollectionModal` already synthesizes pending schedule options (`HandCollectionModal.tsx:250-297`), while the current page receives schedules but lists only PDCs (`HandInstallmentsVaultView.tsx:132-170`). This is the primary product gap.
- Collection routes are different: schedule-only installment collection uses the existing contract/schedule receipt flow (`CashCollectionReceiptModal.tsx:15-29,98-209` and the contract inspector). A PDC can use the existing `HandCollectionModal` and inspector actions. The collection date resolves to an accounting period; locked periods cannot post. Pre-handover receipts credit deferred revenue `203000`; post-handover receipts credit receivables `103000`; cash/bank destination is `101000`/`102000` (`CashCollectionReceiptModal.tsx:59-70,176-193`; `ERPWorkstationContext.tsx:2628-2725`). UI must not calculate or change postings.
- Cheque deposit moves `In Safe` to `Deposited` and journal movement to 104000; clearance changes status/schedule/contract and posts cash/bank; bounce records reversal and, through `handleConfirmBounceCheque`, marks the linked schedule `Defaulted` (`ERPWorkstationContext.tsx:2156-2264,2789-2868`). Multiple status-change paths exist; route each UX action to its correct existing handler rather than simulating transitions in UI state.
- The page header currently says “تسجيل قسط أو سند قبض جديد” but routes to `NewChequeModal`, whose current title is **إضافة ملحق أو دفعة إضافية للعقد** (`HandInstallmentsVaultView.tsx:1197-1205`; `NewChequeModal.tsx:364-368`). Label the action truthfully; do not promise independent receipt creation unless the workflow actually exists.
- There is no verified standalone persisted receipt registry in the page contract. A scheduled/unpaid instrument may be printed as a **سند استحقاق** but must never print as an official **سند قبض** before successful collection. Existing view renders the latter title for `printingPdcItem` regardless of clearance (`HandInstallmentsVaultView.tsx:385-387,1702-1761`); correct presentation requires verified posted state and a real receipt reference.

## 2. Operator questions, answered in order

1. **What needs action today?** Overdue / defaulted, due today, cheque awaiting bank clearance, and next seven days, each with amount and count.
2. **Who and what do I contact?** Buyer, contract, unit/project, tranche or cheque reference, due date, remaining amount, and responsible next action.
3. **What happened already?** Cash received vs cheque only deposited, receipt/posting reference, collection date, and why an item is blocked.
4. **What comes next?** Upcoming dues by date and expected amount, not forecasted cash presented as received cash.

## 3. Default page composition (desktop) — calendar first

Keep the current 50px shell header and docked RTL sidebar. Omit persistent side widgets; the collections workspace itself has two useful columns. The Notion-inspired reference is **a practical month grid with visible dated items and a synchronized side agenda**, not a Notion clone, decorative date picker, or drag-and-drop payment editor.

1. **Compact heading and four factual KPI cards:** `أجندة ومواعيد الأقساط وسندات القبض` with the current business date and scoped actions. Keep the four canonical cards (~100–112px) for overdue, due today, next seven days, and actually collected this month (§5). No sparklines, invented deltas, or five-card strip.
2. **Calendar toolbar:** month/year with previous/next/today controls, `شهر | أسبوع` switch, one search, and a small status/project filter. Default to the **current month** on desktop. Month navigation changes the visible date window and the table's date range (plus unresolved overdue), but never silently changes the meaning of global KPI cards. A date selection narrows the table and opens that day's item list without replacing the separate Today panel; clear selection returns to the visible month. The selected date and filter scopes are always visible.
3. **Primary calendar + `مواعيد اليوم` companion:** a seven-column RTL month grid occupies roughly two thirds of the content width, with a 300–340px Today panel on the left. Week starts Saturday to match Egyptian work context (confirm against product locale before implementation); day labels and weekday order are RTL. Each day cell shows the date, up to two compact real due items (buyer/contract short label + amount), a `+N` overflow control, and a concise due total. Use a subtle date highlight, not colored day-card fills. Accessible list text must expose all items even when the visual cell truncates.
4. **Always-visible Today panel:** heading `مواعيد اليوم` with business date, count and amount; rows show time-free due date context, buyer, unit/contract, amount, and one explicit `عرض`/`تحصيل` action when valid. If the business model has no appointment time field, **do not draw hourly time slots or fabricate meeting hours**. Below today's items, a separately labeled `متأخرات تحتاج متابعة` section displays overdue backlog (not moved into today's date) and a `مودع بانتظار التحصيل` bank-clearance section if present. Empty today means “لا توجد أقساط تستحق اليوم” while overdue remains visible.
5. **Full-width table beneath the calendar:** always rendered, not an alternate view that hides the calendar. The table and calendar use the same deduplicated projection and status/project filters. On load it shows the current month plus unresolved overdue; navigating months updates that range, and clicking a day narrows rows to that day. The Today panel continues showing the actual current business day. Columns: due date, buyer/contract/unit, tranche or cheque, remaining amount, instrument/payment stage, and explicit `عرض التفاصيل`. Paginate without changing calendar or KPI totals.
6. **Visible analytics section below the table:** heading `تحليل الاستحقاقات والتحصيلات`, with the two primary charts in §6 side by side on wide screens and stacked on compact screens. Do not collapse or hide charts by default; they may be below the first viewport because the calendar and Today panel are the operational priority. Charts share the same business date and data projection and state their own date windows.

At 1341px the calendar and Today panel remain side by side only while day cells retain readable date and amount; otherwise Today stacks below the month grid via container query. The month remains available, with a week switch for tighter space. At 390px, show `مواعيد اليوم` first, then a compact week/list agenda by default with an explicit `الشهر` switch; the complete table becomes a readable stacked record list beneath it. No horizontal document overflow or clipped calendar labels.

## 5. Data projection and metric definitions — never double count

Implement one read-only projection of `contracts + schedules + pdcRecords`, rather than separately calculating KPIs, chart series and table data. Stable identity: linked schedule row keyed by `schedule_id`; attach its PDC as an instrument, not as a second collectible. Unlinked PDC is a separate item keyed by `cheque_id`. A schedule without PDC remains visible as a schedule-only due. If a linkage is ambiguous, keep records distinct and flag for reconciliation; never merge by amount/date alone. Exclude `SUPERSEDED`, `Void`, `PDC Void`, and rescinded/inactive contract dues from actionable totals while retaining historical visibility where appropriate. Use decimal arithmetic (`D`) and `max(0, nominal_value - amount_paid)` for outstanding schedule amounts, but do not infer partial-payment support from the type alone.

Four top metrics are disjoint by collection date bucket except the fourth historical metric:

1. **متأخر / متعثر**: open remaining principal with due date before today, including defaulted and bounced-linked dues once each; show count and outstanding amount. A deposited-but-uncleared cheque past due remains unresolved and must not read “collected”.
2. **مستحق اليوم**: due today, outstanding and not cleared; count + amount.
3. **خلال 7 أيام**: due strictly after today through day 7, outstanding; count + amount. Never include overdue/today in this card.
4. **محصل هذا الشهر**: actual collected amount with `paid_date` or `cleared_date` in the current business month, not deposited or merely scheduled. Deduplicate linked schedule/PDC. If a historical cleared item has no reliable collection date, omit it from the timed metric and note the unclassified amount rather than invent a date.

KPI scope is **global for the current business date** while search, selected day and month navigation filter the calendar/table. Clicking a card navigates to its relevant date or queue scope; a visible filtered-result count appears separately. Date comparisons use the business timezone (Egypt) on `YYYY-MM-DD` date-only values, not an accidental mix of local today and UTC `toISOString()`.

Status vocabulary is two axes: **due urgency** (`متأخر`, `اليوم`, `قادم`) and **instrument/payment state** (`مجدول`, `في الخزنة`, `مودع بالبنك`, `محصل`, `مرتد`, `متعثر`). A deposited cheque may be overdue; do not collapse these into one mutually exclusive badge. Use fixed semantic micro-pills; theme accent only for navigation/actions.

## 6. Charts and calendar — visible, truthful analysis

The month calendar is the **primary scheduling visualization** (§3), not one of several optional charts. It displays actual due records and their amounts. The analytics section below the synchronized table is **visible by default** and always contains two chart panels with explicit empty/coverage states; it is never a hidden drawer or a decorative dashboard before the calendar.

1. **Expected dues by week (next 8 weeks):** vertical stacked bars from outstanding *future* due amounts, with separately labeled segments: schedule-only, cheque in safe, deposited awaiting clearance. X-axis calendar weeks, Y-axis EGP, tooltip amount + item count. Clicking a bar moves calendar/table to that week. Excludes collected/void/superseded, and shows a meaningful zero state when no future dues. This is **scheduled exposure**, not a cash forecast or collected revenue.
2. **Actual collections (previous 12 weeks):** weekly bars or line using verified `paid_date` / `cleared_date`, split only if cash/bank method is reliably recorded. Tooltip explicitly says actual collection date and amount. Do not convert expected due date into collection date. If timestamp coverage is incomplete, retain the chart panel with a visible coverage note and plot only verifiable records; if none are verifiable, show its empty state instead of fabricated points.
3. **Overdue aging** is a third compact horizontal breakdown beneath the two primary charts only if it remains legible and adds decision value: 1–7, 8–30, 31–60, 61+ days based on open remaining amounts. Bounced items get a separate exception count, not duplicate money. If analytics space is tight, the two required charts and actionable queue take precedence.

Avoid pie/donut charts of all nominal PDC values and circular “collection rate”: overlapping schedules and instruments and missing timestamps make those denominators misleading.

## 7. States, motion and copy

- **Empty dataset:** explain that a contract's payment plan creates scheduled dues; direct user to contracts (not “record a receipt” for a payment that does not exist). **Filtered empty:** show applied filters and one reset action. **Loading/error:** skeleton for the table and honest retry message; never replace missing data with fake figures.
- **Blocked action:** show why (already cleared; void/superseded; locked period; no linked contract; cheque not in a valid transition), without presenting a success-looking control. Disable while posting and keep the drawer open with an error if persistence fails. Re-fetch the authoritative record after success; only then offer receipt print.
- **Motion:** 140–180ms tab/hover/focus feedback; 180–220ms month/week or selected-day transition that preserves date context; 220–260ms drawer entry/exit (`transform` + `opacity`). No animated balances, rolling numbers, bouncing badges, staggered dashboard entrances, or calendar cards jumping on data refresh. `prefers-reduced-motion` makes state changes immediate.
- **Arabic copy:** formal, task-oriented (`مستحق اليوم`, `في الخزنة`, `أودع بالبنك`, `تم التحصيل`, `شيك مرتد`). Avoid colloquial `النهاردة`, `لسه ما اتحصلش`, `الميعاد`, and calling an unpaid schedule a receipt. Numerals and currency use tabular figures and accessible contrast.

## 9. Out of scope without separate business approval

New receipt database/table, arbitrary partial collection, changing ledger posting accounts, automated bulk posting, fabricated historical collections, a new design language, or a global redesign of unrelated FIN-OS screens. The type system admits `Partially Paid`, but current hand collection marks a linked schedule `Paid` with the submitted amount (`ERPWorkstationContext.tsx:2689-2725`); do not present partial settlement as supported until the domain flow is specified and verified.
