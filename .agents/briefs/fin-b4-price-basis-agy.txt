DEFINITIONS (approved by the user)
- Project sales value = sum of gross_contract_value of live contracts (status <> 'Rescinded') on the project + catalog price (price_egp + tax_amount_egp) of every unit with no live contract. For a non-building or a building with no units: its contract value if contracted, else property price_egp.
- Contracted sales = sum of gross_contract_value of live contracts. Never catalog prices.
- Cost = net effective cost (calculateCostItemEffectiveTotals(item).netEffectiveCost from zakaria-farid/src/lib/erp/propertyCostEngine.ts), the same basis as calculatePropertyAuditMetrics.
- Margin = (sales value − cost) / sales value.
