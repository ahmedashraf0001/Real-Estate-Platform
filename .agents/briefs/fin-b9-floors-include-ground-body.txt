TASK fin-b9-floors-include-ground (FINANCIAL_CORE: unit count drives per-unit prices)

GOAL
The admin building form has "عدد الأدوار" (total_floors) and units_per_floor. The floor plan and the saved units disagree:
- Floor plan: zakaria-farid/src/lib/layering/instances.ts buildBuildingStructureAndUnits (~322-350) draws a ground floor with no flats plus typicalFloors = Math.min(14, Math.max(1, totalFloors - 1)) residential floors.
- Saved units: zakaria-farid/src/app/actions/properties.ts calls buildBuildingUnits with totalFloors = total_floors, so it saves total_floors × units_per_floor units.
Live repro: total_floors 3, units_per_floor 2. The plan shows Flat 1A–2B (4 flats). The DB saved 1A–3B (6 units).

RULE ([user-confirmed 2026-10-07], PROJECT_SPEC.md "Partner payouts and final pricing")
"عدد الأدوار" includes the ground floor, and the ground floor has no units. Saved units = (total floors − 1) × units per floor, numbered from floor 1. A 1-floor building keeps one residential floor (same as the plan generator: Math.max(1, totalFloors - 1)).
