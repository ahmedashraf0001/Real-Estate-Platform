GOAL
Editing a cost item's total in its 24-hour window keeps the old payable tranches. Live repro: item c1729fd2 edited 1,000,001 -> 1,000,000; total_cost_egp and remaining_amount_egp are 1,000,000, but its 7 payable_installments still sum to 1,000,001.

RULE ([user-confirmed 2026-10-07], PROJECT_SPEC.md "Partner payouts and final pricing")
When a cost item's total is edited, its new remaining amount is re-split evenly over its unpaid tranches; the last unpaid tranche absorbs rounding. Paid and partly paid amounts never change.
